Cleaning different types of London homes

A domestic cleaning visit can look quite different depending on the property. A modern apartment may need more attention around glass, contemporary bathrooms and kitchen surfaces, while an older London property may have wooden floors, fireplaces, decorative features or other finishes that require a gentler approach.

Shared houses can also need more time in communal kitchens and bathrooms, while family homes may benefit from regular attention to high-use living areas, bedrooms and floors.

If your property contains delicate surfaces, original features or rooms you want prioritised, tell us when booking so the available cleaning time can be planned more effectively.

Choosing the right domestic cleaning service

Regular cleaning is suitable when you want ongoing weekly or fortnightly support and prefer your home to be maintained consistently.

One-off cleaning is useful when you do not need a recurring cleaner but want help before visitors, after a busy period or whenever the home needs an occasional reset.

Deep cleaning is better suited to properties that require substantially more detailed work, particularly in kitchens, bathrooms, fixtures, edges and areas where dirt has built up over time.
