When to book

Ideal after renovations, decorating and refurbishment

After builders cleaning is useful once the main building or decorating work has been completed and the contractors have removed their tools and larger waste. Fine dust can remain on floors, skirting, shelves, worktops and other surfaces even when the property appears generally tidy.

A professional post-build clean helps remove that remaining residue and prepares the property for normal use. It can be especially valuable before moving furniture back into rooms, welcoming guests, taking property photographs or handing a refurbished property over to tenants or owners.

Who it is for

After builders cleaning for homes and properties across London

Our service is suitable for homeowners completing renovations, landlords preparing refurbished rental properties, tenants after improvement work and property professionals who need a clean, presentable space after contractors have finished.

Every property is different, so we assess the level of dust, the number of rooms and the overall condition before confirming the most suitable cleaning approach. Larger refurbishment projects or properties with heavier residue may require additional time compared with a smaller decorating job.
