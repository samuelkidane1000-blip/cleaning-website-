A cleaning service that fits around Chelsea households

Regular cleaning works well for households that prefer a predictable weekly or fortnightly routine rather than arranging separate visits every time. It can also make each visit more efficient because the property is being maintained consistently instead of repeatedly brought back from a heavily used condition.

One-off cleaning is better suited to occasional situations: preparing the home before visitors, refreshing a property after a particularly busy period or giving certain rooms more attention than they receive during normal day-to-day cleaning.

For properties requiring considerably more work, deep cleaning gives the cleaner additional time to concentrate on built-up dirt, detailed kitchen and bathroom work, edges, fixtures and other areas that fall outside a straightforward maintenance visit.

What we can clean during a Chelsea home visit

The exact tasks depend on the service booked and the condition of the property, but typical domestic cleaning can cover kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living rooms, hallways, floors, accessible surfaces and general dusting and tidying.

If there is something particular you want prioritised — for example a kitchen that needs more attention, bathrooms that require a deeper clean, or specific rooms that matter most before guests arrive — include that information when making your booking.
