Working around period features

A large share of Chelsea's stock is Victorian and Edwardian townhouses, many converted into flats but keeping original cornicing, fireplaces and wood or parquet flooring. That changes the job: no harsh chemicals on old wood or marble, more careful handling around original fittings, and generally more time per room than a comparable new-build. If your property has original features, flag it at booking so we send someone used to that kind of work.

We also cover the newer developments closer to Chelsea Harbour, which are a faster, more standard clean by comparison.

Chelsea cleaning FAQs

Do you work with the building's existing cleaner or porter?
Yes — a number of Chelsea mansion blocks have a porter or communal cleaner for shared areas. We coordinate access with them where needed rather than duplicating their work.

Can you handle period features like original fireplaces or wood floors?
Yes, our deep cleaning service is set up for this — let us know at booking so we bring the right products and allow enough time.

How quickly can I get a one-off clean before a short let starts?
Usually within 48 hours, though turnaround is tighter in peak letting periods — book as early as you can if the changeover date is fixed.
